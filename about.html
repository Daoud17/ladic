GET TO KNOW US

Committed to Care, Dignity & Inclusion.

At LADIC, we believe every person deserves respect, opportunity, and the support needed to live with dignity and independence.

Our work focuses on supporting people with disabilities through an inclusive and person-centered approach. We recognize that every individual has unique abilities, aspirations, and needs.

By bringing together disability inclusion, rehabilitation, family support, and community participation, we aim to create meaningful opportunities and a more accessible society.

Person-Centered Care

Recognizing each individual's unique needs and abilities.

Equal Opportunities

Encouraging accessibility, independence, and participation.

Community Inclusion

Building stronger connections and a sense of belonging.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by compassion, inclusion, and respect, we work toward a future where every person has the opportunity to thrive.

01 / OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

To support and empower people with disabilities through compassionate, person-centered care, rehabilitation, and inclusive opportunities that promote dignity, independence, and meaningful participation in society.

Empowering Every Ability
02 / OUR FUTURE

Our Vision

To contribute to an inclusive and accessible society where people of all abilities are valued, respected, and empowered to reach their full potential, with equal access to opportunities, care, and community life.

Building an Inclusive Tomorrow
THE PRINCIPLES THAT GUIDE US

Our Core Values

Our values shape the way we approach care, support individuals and families, and promote a more inclusive community.

01

Dignity & Respect

We believe every person deserves to be treated with dignity, kindness, and respect, regardless of their abilities or circumstances.

02

Compassion & Care

We approach every individual and family with empathy, understanding, and a genuine commitment to their well-being.

03

Inclusion & Equality

We encourage equal opportunities, accessibility, and meaningful participation for people of all abilities in society.

04

Empowerment

We support individuals in developing their abilities, building confidence, and pursuing greater independence in everyday life.

BEYOND CARE

Building a Community Where Everyone Belongs.

True inclusion goes beyond individual support. It means creating environments where every person feels welcomed, valued, and empowered.

At LADIC, we recognize the importance of families, communities, and accessible spaces in supporting people with disabilities. Our approach encourages meaningful connections, participation, and opportunities for greater independence.

Supporting Families

Encouraging family involvement, understanding, and ongoing support.

Promoting Accessibility

Advocating for environments and opportunities that welcome all abilities.

Encouraging Participation

Helping people build connections and engage in community life.
